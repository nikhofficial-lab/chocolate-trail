Accepting customer orders

This is the master switch. Pausing does not alter stock, campaigns or existing orders.

Operating rules

These are deliberate safety limits.

  • Never delete an order. Cancel it here so stock and coupon use are reversed once.
  • Never subtract stock manually after a successful website order.
  • New coupons are created paused. Review the margin before activating them.
  • TWINBIT remains locked to its current 10% customer / 0% referrer server rule until a separate tested migration changes that rule.

Product stock

Add a delivery, save an exact physical count, or mark one product sold out. Every change is recorded.

Recent orders

Cancellation restores every line item and reverses one coupon use atomically. A paid referral reward must be manually resolved first.

Campaign schedule

Current coupons and referrals
